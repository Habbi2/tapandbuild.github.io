## 🏭 Build Your Factory Empire

**Tap & Build** is the ultimate idle clicker tycoon. Start with a single tap and grow into a cosmic factory empire. Buy machines, hire managers, invest in the live player stock market, and prestige for permanent multipliers. Your factories never sleep — earn offline even while you're away!

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚙️ **32 unique machines** across 8 tiers
- 🌟 **Prestige & Ascension** perk systems
- 📈 **Live player stock market** — invest in other players!
- 🏆 **Global leaderboards** & seasonal competition
- 🎯 **Daily challenges** & offline earnings
- 👔 **15 unique managers** to automate production

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Tap** the big button to earn cash
2. **Buy machines** to generate passive income
3. **Hire managers** so machines run automatically
4. **Prestige** to earn permanent multipliers
5. **Invest** in the live market to grow your wealth
6. **Compete** on seasonal leaderboards
