TAP & BUILD - DATA DELETION REQUEST
=====================================

HOW TO REQUEST DATA DELETION:
1. Send an email to: support@tapandbuild.com
2. Subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
3. Include your Player Name from the game
4. We will confirm within 48 hours and delete within 30 days

DATA DELETED:
- Player name and leaderboard scores
- Cloud save data (game progress)
- Device ID and analytics data
- Crash logs

DATA RETAINED (legal requirements):
- Purchase history (7 years for tax/accounting)
- Anonymized crash logs (90 days)

For EU users: This complies with GDPR Article 17.
