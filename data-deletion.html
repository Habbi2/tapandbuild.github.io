How to Request Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of all personal data associated with your use of Tap & Build. We process all deletion requests within 30 days.

1 Send an email to our support team with the subject line: Data Deletion Request
2 Include your Player Name as shown in the game's leaderboard or settings screen. This helps us identify your data.
3 We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive a final confirmation email.
📧 support@tapandbuild.com

What Data Is Deleted vs Retained

Player name & leaderboard scores ✓ Deleted
Cloud save data (game progress) ✓ Deleted
Device ID & analytics data ✓ Deleted
Purchase history ⊘ Retained (legal requirement, 7 years)
Crash logs (anonymized) ⊘ Retained (anonymized, 90 days)

Important Notes

  • Data deletion is irreversible. Your game progress, leaderboard scores, and purchases will be permanently lost.
  • If you have made in-app purchases, we are legally required to retain transaction records for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Deletion requests are processed free of charge.
  • For EU users: This process complies with GDPR Article 17 (Right to erasure).
